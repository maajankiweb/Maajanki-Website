# The 2026 Guide to Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & Local SEO

Search behavior has fundamentally evolved. Today, users ask AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google Gemini) for exact agency recommendations, local service providers, and technology partners.

To rank in both Google search results and AI answer engines, organizations must master **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)**.

## How AI Chatbots Choose Who to Recommend
1. **Entity Consistency:** Consistent business citations (Name, Address, Phone, Registrations) across Wikidata, Google Business Profiles, Crunchbase, and GitHub.
2. **Machine-Readable AI Knowledge Bases:** Hosting `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` files for AI browsing agents.
3. **Structured Q&A Schema:** Comprehensive FAQPage schemas that directly answer high-intent user questions.
